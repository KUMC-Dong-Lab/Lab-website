Research

Clostridioides difficile pathogenesis

Mechanisms of virulence variability, phase variation, and regulatory switches associated with disease severity.

  • Focus: flagellar gene regulation & inversion switches
  • Approach: genetics, molecular biology, microscopy, infection models

Pathogen-commensal interactions

Mechanisms of how C. difficile outcompete intestinal commensal bacterial species for infection, and how we may leverage avirulent strains as probiotics.

  • Focus: strain-strain competition, metabolism
  • Approach: metabolomics & transcriptomics, infection models

Host–pathogen interactions

Interactions that modulate infection outcomes and therapeutic opportunities.

  • Focus: immune modulation & colonization dynamics
  • Collab: clinical & translational partners
